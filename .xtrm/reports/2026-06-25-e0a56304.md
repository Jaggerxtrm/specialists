on PATH; the container model resolves this by baking `gh` into a deterministic image.

### Architectural decisions embedded
- Phase 6 telemetry decouples from a new JSON field: the specialist's existing
  Forensic.v1 `trace_id` is the durable handle, surfaced in the PR body.
- `requires_worktree:true` makes the specialist's commits staged in isolation
  before Phase 6 pushes — aligns with the container's per-repo lock + fork model.
- `external_commands: ["gh"]` is kept as a fatal gate deliberately: in the
  deterministic container, fail-fast at pre-run is preferable to a mid-Phase-6
  crash.
