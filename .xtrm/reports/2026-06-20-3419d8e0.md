## Summary

Shipped the full `sp console` multi-repo discovery feature in three back-to-back waves plus a cosmetic alignment of `sp ps` to the TUI's row style. Wave C (`unitAI-fd4pl`, PR #143) bumped the discovery walk to depth-2 with a stat-only worktree skip (`.git` exists and is a file → worktree/submodule → skip subtree); the skip must precede the marker check because `resolveObservabilityDbLocation` follows `git rev-parse --git-common-dir` and would false-positive every worktree. Final piece (`unitAI-ugw4s`, PR #144) wired `sp ps` leaf rows through `renderJobRow` and the bottom stats line through `renderStatsLine` so the CLI and TUI share status icons, column order, palette, and ctx coloring while the CLI retains its prefix-based tree and system-health block. Live smoke from `~/projects/mercury` discovered 9 nested sub-repos automatically post-Wave-C; 271/271 console tests + 21/21 ps tests green throughout. All four PRs admin-merged after pre-existing-on-master OSV scan was confirmed not regressed.
